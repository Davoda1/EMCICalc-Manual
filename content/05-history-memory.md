**Ans** is the previous confirmed result. The memory controls are:

| Control | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| MS | Store the result in M |
| MR | Recall M |
| M+ | Add the result to M |
| MC | Clear M |

R0–R9 are independent named registers. Open the register panel in Toolbox to inspect and manage their stored values. Empty is distinct from a register holding zero. Insert the chosen register into an expression when you want to reuse it; inspecting a value does not evaluate the calculator.

Open **History** or press **Ctrl+H**. History retains up to 10,000 successful entries, with expressions and their saved results. Recall reuses recorded data; deliberately evaluating the recalled expression is a new calculation. Use history's available search, copy and export controls to locate or preserve results.

The **Details** workbench inspects a selected result. Saved results retain the calculation context needed to interpret values such as Ans, M and registers. Workbench inspection and insertion are distinct from confirming a new calculation in Calculate.
